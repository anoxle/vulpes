About this Discord permissions calculator

Paste any bot invite link or client ID to see exactly what the bot can do. Toggle permissions, start from a preset, and copy a fresh invite link. Everything runs in your browser.

What is a Discord permissions integer?

It is a single number that stores every permission a bot asks for. Each permission is one bit, and the invite link carries the sum of them in its permissions parameter.

How do I generate a Discord bot invite link with custom permissions?

Paste a bot invite link or client ID, pick a preset or toggle individual permissions, then copy the Updated Invite Link.

Is the Administrator permission safe to grant?

Administrator gives a bot every permission and skips all channel overrides. Only grant it to bots you fully trust.

Does this tool store my invite links?

No. Everything is calculated in your browser, and nothing you paste is sent to a server.
