Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10/30/2026 · 2 min read

1. Introduction

Welcome to the Vulpes Privacy Policy ("Policy" or "policy"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you interact with our Discord bot, Vulpes (the "Bot"). By using the Bot, you agree to the collection and use of your information in accordance with this policy. If you do not, don't use the Bot please.

2. Information We Collect

In order to provide and improve our services, we may collect and process the following information:

  • User IDs
  • Command Usage

When errors occur while using a command, we may log the command name, associated User ID, and the exact date / time for debugging.

3. How We Use Your Information

We are committed to using your collected data respectfully and fairly.

Usage of User IDs:

  1. General database purposes.
  2. Blacklisting and abuse prevention.

Usage of Command Usage:

  1. Providing and improving our services.
  2. Identifying and getting rid of issues such as errors.

4. How We Keep Your Data Protected

To ensure your collected data is safe, we follow these practices.

  • Everything is stored in secure environments.
  • Access to data is strictly limited to Managers.
  • Your data is encrypted and protected before going anywhere.

We use reasonable security measures such as encryption and obfuscation methods where appropriate.

5. How Long We Keep Your Data

We keep your collected data for only as long as it's necessary to provide the Bot's services and maintain abuse prevention systems.

Users may request for their data to be deleted at any time.

6. Important Notice

If you are blacklisted; your User ID will be kept in the blacklisted user database and removed from all other databases. This data is kept for at least 3–4 years or until you are unblacklisted via a successful appeal.

7. AI Privacy

By using commands that are powered by Artificial Intelligence, you shall acknowledge and agree that any content you provide may be used by our third-party AI providers to improve their services, according to their respective policies.

We use more than one provider, and we are committed to picking the best one that has the most privacy to ensure nothing sensitive is being sent.

Entire section can be ignored for small AI-powered commands since they are local models ran by us.

8. Sharing Your Information

We absolutely do not sell, trade, or rent your collected data to any third parties. We will only share information if:

  1. Required to comply with a valid legal obligation / Responding to lawful law enforcement requests.

9. Your Rights & Contact

You reserve the right to:

  1. Request for removal of your collected data.
  2. Request for access to the data we store about you.

Some collected data may be encrypted or obfuscated for security purposes.

  1. Ask for clarification on how your data is used.

To make such request, please join our Discord.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. All changes will be announced in our Support Server. If you keep using the Bot after changes are posted, it means you agree and accept the new Policy.
