Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12/24/2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to the Vulpes Privacy Policy. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Discord bot, Vulpes (the "Bot"). By using the Bot, you automatically agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not, please refrain from using Vulpes.

2. Information We Collect

In order to provide and improve our services, we may collect and process the following information:

  • User IDs
  • Command Usage

When errors occur while using commands, we may log the command name, associated User ID, and timestamp for debugging

3. How We Use Your Information

We use User IDs for:

  • General database purposes (private)
  • Blacklisting and abuse prevention (private)

We use Command Usage for:

  • Providing and improving our services.
  • Identifying and resolving issues / errors

4. How Your Data Is Protected

To ensure your information is safe, we follow these practices:

  • Everything is stored in secure environments
  • Access to data is strictly limited to authorized managers only.
  • Your data is obfuscated and protected before going anywhere

We use reasonable security measures such as encryption and obfuscation where appropriate.

5. How Long We Keep Your Data

We keep your information for only as long as it's necessary to provide the Bot's services and maintain abuse prevention systems

  • Users may request data deletion at any time

6. Important Note

If you are blacklisted, your UserID will be kept only in the blacklisting database and removed from all other databases. This data is kept for at least 4 years or until you are unblacklisted via a successful appeal.

7. AI Privacy

By using commands powered by AI, you acknowledge and agree that any content you provide may be used by our third-party AI providers to improve their services, according to their respective policies.

We use more than one AI provider.

8. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your information to any third parties. We will only share information if:

  • Required to comply with a valid legal obligation
  • Responding to lawful law enforcement requests

9. Your Rights & Contact

You may request:

  • Removal of your data
  • Access to the data we store about you

Some stored information may be encrypted or obfuscated for security purposes.

  • Clarification on how your data is used

To make a request, please join our Discord.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Updates will be posted on this page and you will get notified in our Support Server(s). Continued use of the bot means you accept the update Policy.
